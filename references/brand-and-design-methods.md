# Brand and design methods: the Marketing Engineer Agent Skill Pack and the FDE course

Every rule that applies to the Watcher's brand identity, design language, logo and visual style, with its source.

- **SP** = `/Users/daryldimitrianthony/FDE Course/3 Skills/agent-skill-pack/`. The pack is written in Dutch; terms are
  translated here, with the original in parentheses.
- **FDE** = `/Users/daryldimitrianthony/FDE Course/`.

Neither source defines a logo system or colour palette. The closest are the platform consistency rules and the course's
"four decisions".

## 1. Brand and perception (SP `02-onboarding/SKILL.md`)

- **Phase 2, Brand & Perception (L63-74):**
  - How do you *want* to be perceived (feelings, associations, status)?
  - What stops people from buying?
  - Who shapes the market's worldview?
- **Phase 4, Trust Signals (L90-98):** claims you can credibly make, with specific numbers.
- **Phase 5, Founder (L102-113):** the "messy, real" origin story and a contrarian belief. That belief becomes a
  positioning tool.
- **The `/brand-voice` template (L1043-1197):**
  - Recognition Factor: "the ONE element that makes this voice instantly identifiable".
  - Formatting & Visual Rhythm.
  - Platform adaptations.
- **Applied to the Watcher** (`docs/marketing/references/brand-voice.md`):
  - Personality: "the friend at the dinner party who actually knows how Marktplaats works… calm, practical, a bit dry.
    Never sells, never hypes."
  - Recognition factor: "every claim is concrete and every alert explains itself."

## 2. Positioning (SP `03-strategist/SKILL.md`, `knowledge-base/frameworks.md`)

- **Market awareness** (Marktbewustzijn, L118-135). Our buyer is solution- to product-aware (research.md). Imagery
  should therefore show the product and its mechanism, not just an identity mood.
- **Market sophistication** (Marktverfijning, L137-149):
  - Stage 3: a **new mechanism**. Stage 4: refine the mechanism. Stage 5: identity and personal brand.
  - Marktplaats alerts sit at stages 3-4, so **the mechanism is the brand**: every listing gets a score from 0 to 10 and
    one line on *why*.
- **Value Equation** (L79-100): Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood) / (Time Delay × Effort).
  - "Perception is reality."
  - Visuals should raise **perceived likelihood** through proof: a real scored card, 9/10 against 0/10.
  - Visuals should also show that **effort** is low: one sentence.
- **Five components** (L49-77): the Plan/Mechanism follows the Law of Three, so three named steps. Ours is **Say it ·
  Pick when · Get only the good ones**.
- **Three dimensions of the prospect** (L169-182), identification: "show the type of person who uses your product" (a
  calm, smart buyer, not a reseller or sniper).
- **Clarity audit** (fw L1072-1082): "Can someone understand in 10 seconds what they get?"
- **MAGIC naming** (L307-319): for named mechanisms or features. It is not needed for the product name, which is kept.

## 3. Copy, taglines and the big idea (SP `05-copywriter`)

- **Core principle** (SKILL L59-63): copy channels existing desire, it doesn't create it.
- **Rule of One** (L613-620): one big idea, one emotion, one benefit.
- **Four U's** (`references/long-form-frameworks/leads.md` L129-140): Urgent, Unique, Useful, Ultra-specific.
- **Unique Mechanism, "transubstantiated"** (`frameworks.md` L161-174): proprietary naming makes the ordinary
  extraordinary.
- **Demonstrate / Before-and-after** (leads.md L144-180). This is the strongest visual device for us: a docking station
  scored 0/10 and skipped, next to a Mac mini scored 9/10 and e-mailed.
- **Salespages** (`knowledge-base/salespage-systeem.md` L49): "4-step process as a visual element".

## 4. Visual rules for content (SP `06-content-creator`)

- **LinkedIn** (`knowledge-base/linkedin/writing-rules.md` L86-102):
  - Visual types: stat card, screenshot, mini infographic, before/after, personal photo.
  - "No visual is better than a bad visual."
  - "The visual must work on its own."
  - "Use visuals you can actually make."
- **Carousel** (`platform-guides/linkedin.md` L106-131):
  - 7-12 slides at 1080×1350 (4:5).
  - Brand colours used consistently; bold type of at least 24pt; 5-7 lines per slide at most; numbering "3/10".
  - The **first slide works on its own in the feed**.
  - Infographics beat stock photos and selfies.
- **Visual branding** (`platform-guides/instagram.md` L77-81):
  - **3-5 brand colours at most**, **1-2 fonts**.
  - Templates for recognition.
  - Professional photos over stock over AI-generated.
  - Mistake #1 is "no consistent visual style".
- **Template-to-visual pairing** (`linkedin/templates.md` L1649-1686), for example: stat card, before/after, logo grid,
  screenshot, comparison graphic.
- **Quality gates:**
  - the "Only You" filter: could only this person have made it?
  - the anti-boring checklist: at least 3 of 5.

## 5. Ad and creative specs (SP `07-ads-specialist`)

- **Formats:**
  - single image 1080×1080 or 1200×628, with at most 20% text and contrasting colours
  - carousel of 2-10 cards: first card is the hook, last is the CTA
  - video 1:1 or 9:16: the first 3 seconds are the hook, subtitles are required
- **Pattern interrupts** (`knowledge-base/gedeeld/ad-psychologie.md` L176-196): an unexpected colour on a blue/white
  feed, and a before/after split.
- **Creative brief** (SKILL L301-337): tone of voice comes from the vault's brand voice.

## 6. Product and presentation (SP `10-product-builder`, `09-seo-specialist`)

- **Transformation test** (`knowledge-base/product-validatie.md` L17-75): who, the transformation in one sentence, why
  you, why now.
- **Differentiation axes** (L150-159): method, format, experience.
- **SEO** (`09-seo-specialist`): alt text on every image; Open Graph title and description written separately to win
  social clicks.

## 7. FDE course rules

- **Four decisions "that stop demos looking amateur"** (FDE `3 Skills/fde-skill/references/software-engineering-foundation.md`
  L108-113, and Day 1 slide 7.4):
  - **one spacing scale (8/16/24/32/48)**: "Never 13 or 27"
  - **two fonts, five sizes**: "more reads as chaos"
  - **one accent colour and one neutral ramp**
  - real loading, empty and error states
- **Design process** (Day 1 full deck, block 18):
  - The 12 steps end with visual design → accessibility → **design system**.
  - Tokens (colour, type, spacing and radius as named values), components, patterns. "No new colours without a reason."
- **Accessibility:** contrast 4.5:1 (3:1 for large type), keyboard-only use, labels and alt text, reduced motion, screen
  readers.
- **Demo** (checklist §5, SKILL Phase 5):
  - "Hook lands in 2–3 seconds (message, not architecture)."
  - "A customer cannot judge your architecture. They judge your empty state."
- **Pitch** (checklist §9): the idea in one sentence a non-technical sponsor would repeat back correctly.
- **Day 3, the startup cycle:**
  - The **Hipster** role "owns design, brand and the experience".
  - **Trademark** (merkrecht) protects the name, logo and slogan: "Search first — rebranding after traction is
    expensive."
  - **Differentiator**: "a capability, not a claim; true, verifiable in a demo, hard to copy."
  - The FDE default stack starts with a **Claude Design mockup** before Next.js (fde-skill SKILL L49-52).
