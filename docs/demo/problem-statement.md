# The problem, in one paragraph (no technology words)

People who hunt for a specific second-hand item on Marktplaats, like a Mac mini with enough memory or a Gazelle bike
near home, lose the good ones because they can't keep refreshing the site all day. The site's own saved search e-mails at most
once a day, and most of what it finds isn't the item at all: chargers, docking stations, look-alikes, even a
network switch when you searched for a computer. Today they either check by hand several times a day or accept that
the best listings are gone before they see them. Better looks like this: say once, in your own words, what you want
and when to look, and hear only about new listings that are really worth a look, each with one sentence on why.
