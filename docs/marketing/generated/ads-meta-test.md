## 3-2-2 test protocol
1. **Three hooks:** A, B, C in each language at **€5–10/day per variant** if Daryl approves. Keep language-specific results separate. A full six-creative run is €30–60/day; Daryl may stagger languages to cap spend. Hold audience, destination, placement and budget steady enough to compare hooks. Measure 3-second video view rate, outbound CTR, landing views, sign-ups, watch saves and first alerts where consented measurement exists.
2. **Two bodies:** Keep the strongest *observed* hook in each language and compare a problem-focused body against a screen-demo body. Keep terms and CTA constant.
3. **Two CTAs:** On the winning body, compare “Maak een watch / Create a watch” with “Bekijk de demo / Watch the demo”; both must lead to the promised destination.

**Kill / scale:** Before launch, Daryl sets a maximum test spend and a minimum useful sample. Pause a variant if it has spent the agreed cap without a watch save, if the landing or consent flow is broken, or if comments reveal a misleading claim. Do not declare a winner from one click. Compare cost per saved watch and, later, rated first alerts; scale only a variant with enough observed saves, accurate terms and an acceptable cost against Daryl's preset budget. Increase spend gradually and stop if cost per saved watch worsens beyond the preset limit. No unsupported benchmark CPA is supplied.

## Dutch Advertising Code / publication checklist
- [ ] “Advertentie” or unmistakable paid placement; Daryl's role as maker is clear.
- [ ] Free terms in both languages: first 100 accounts, 30 days, €1 AI budget; capacity read live, no stale number or countdown.
- [ ] No “instant”, guaranteed deal, savings, income or unverified testimonial claim.
- [ ] If evaluation numbers appear, label them 10 October offline test, sample and missed matches; verify against [report](../../../evals/report.md).
- [ ] Independent of Marktplaats; no official affiliation implied by logo or wording.
- [ ] Privacy and cookie information visible before sign-up; pixel waits for valid consent. UTM values contain no personal data.
- [ ] Recheck Meta's current ad rules and [Dutch government advertising guidance](https://business.gov.nl/regulations/advertising/) at launch.
