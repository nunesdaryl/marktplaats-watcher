# Screen demo VSL: recording script (draft, 10 October 2026)

Daryl records his own voice one sentence at a time, leaving a pause between lines; cut the pauses by hand. Aim for 60–90 seconds per language at a natural pace. Record the real app and a real, permission-safe alert; if no qualifying alert is available, label the alert footage as a recorded example and do not imply that it arrived during the take. The proof number is from the [10 October offline evaluation](../../../evals/report.md), not live performance. Vinod's quote is from the [pitch transcript](../PITCH-DECK-TRANSCRIPT.md); it expresses interest, not a purchase.

## Nederlands: teleprompter (about 70–80 seconds)
| Beat | One sentence per recording take |
|---|---|
| Hook | “Ik zocht een Mac mini met 16GB, niet alles met ‘Mac’ in de titel.” |
| Problem | “Dus bleef ik Marktplaats verversen, en de goede waren altijd net weg.” |
| Setup | “Nu beschrijf ik gewoon wat ik zoek, in mijn eigen woorden, en kies ik hoe vaak hij kijkt.” |
| Save | “Ik controleer het voorstel en klik zelf op ‘Save watch’.” |
| Alert | “Verschijnt er een nieuwe advertentie die past, dan krijg ik een e-mail met een score en de reden waarom.” |
| Act | “Is hij goed, dan helpt hij me met een eerste bod, en met één tik staat het klaar op Marktplaats.” |
| Proof | “In onze test met 53 echte advertenties was elke ‘heel goede match’-mail ook echt raak, en hij ving ongeveer drie op de vier.” |
| Human proof | “Cursusdocent Vinod Kumar Bhovi zei op de demodag: ‘I pay 10 euros to get this application … I think I can be your first user.’” |
| Honest limit | “Hij kijkt hoogstens elk kwartier, kan soms een match missen, en is niet van Marktplaats.” |
| CTA | “De eerste honderd mensen krijgen dertig dagen gratis; kijk hoeveel plekken er nog zijn en maak je eigen watch.” |

## English: teleprompter (about 70–80 seconds)
| Beat | One sentence per recording take |
|---|---|
| Hook | “I wanted a Mac mini with 16GB, not everything with ‘Mac’ in the title.” |
| Problem | “So I kept refreshing Marktplaats, and the good ones were always just gone.” |
| Setup | “Now I just describe what I want, in my own words, and choose how often it checks.” |
| Save | “I review the suggestion and press Save watch myself.” |
| Alert | “When a new listing fits, I get an email with a score and the reason why.” |
| Act | “If it's good, it helps me with an opening offer, and one tap gets it ready on Marktplaats.” |
| Proof | “In our test with 53 real listings, every ‘great match’ email was a real match, and it caught about three in four.” |
| Human proof | “At demo day, course instructor Vinod Kumar Bhovi said, ‘I pay 10 euros to get this application … I think I can be your first user.’” |
| Honest limit | “It checks at most every fifteen minutes, can sometimes miss a match, and isn't made by Marktplaats.” |
| CTA | “The first hundred people get thirty days free; see how many places are left and set your own watch.” |

Proof wording maps to the 10 October report: great-only precision 100% (median of 3 runs) and recall 74% (“about three in four”). The station rewrote the opening in plain words on 10 Oct 2026; the earlier precision/recall opening is hook 3 below.

## Three alternate opening hooks (record in both languages)
1. NL: “Deze advertentie kreeg een score en een reden, zodat ik zelf kon beslissen.” EN: “This listing came with a score and a reason, so I could decide for myself.”
2. NL: “Ik zocht een Mac mini met 16GB, niet zomaar alles met ‘Mac’ in de titel.” EN: “I wanted a Mac mini with 16GB, not everything with ‘Mac’ in the title.”
3. NL: “Mijn offline test liet goede meldingen zien, maar ook gemiste matches; hier is hoe de watch werkt.” EN: “My offline test found good alerts and missed matches; here's how the watch works.”

## Slide and screen plan
Fixed style: off-white `#fbfaf7`, lilac highlight on roughly one-third of the words, robot icon before “Marktplaats Watcher”, no more than four lines per slide, ellipsis `…` for continued thoughts. Slides follow the approved spoken transcript; do not manufacture a customer screenshot.

| Time | Visual | Capture / edit note |
|---|---|---|
| 0–8 s | Slide: “A Mac mini with 16GB… not everything with ‘Mac’ in the title.” | Hook; then a fast cut to a crowded Marktplaats results page (public page, no seller data). |
| 8–16 s | Slide: “The good ones were always just gone…” | Problem beat; keep it short. |
| 16–32 s | Screen: type a Mac mini 16GB, €500 watch request. | Mask account and seller data; capture suggestion before Save. |
| 32–42 s | Screen: review schedule and bar, press Save. | Capture the actual confirmation; no jump cut that implies automatic saving. |
| 42–56 s | Screen: real recorded alert email, score and reason. | Blur email address and listing seller; label older footage “recorded alert”. |
| 56–64 s | Screen: “Help me make an offer” → “Bid €X on Marktplaats” | Show the offer sheet; open the listing but never submit a bid. Then the proof slide: “53 real listings · every great-match e-mail was a real match · caught about 3 in 4” (footer: 10 Oct 2026 test, median of 3 runs). |
| 64–74 s | Slide: attributed Vinod quote, source “demo day”. | Plain text, no paid-customer label. |
| 74–90 s | Slide: robot icon + Marktplaats Watcher + offer and live-capacity CTA. | Say terms; no fixed places-left count. |

Record slides while edited voice plays to create A-roll. Add restrained animation or B-roll only at the marked transitions after the audio cut. Save the resulting edit settings as a reusable style note after Daryl approves the first cut.

## Short cut-downs
At a brisk but natural read, these are approximately 15 and 30 seconds. Check timing from Daryl's recorded take and trim pauses, not disclosures.

**15 s NL:** “Ik stel een watch in voor een Mac mini met 16GB. Ik kies mijn schema en klik zelf op Save. Nieuwe matches krijgen een score en reden per mail. De eerste check is stil.”

**15 s EN:** “I set a watch for a Mac mini with 16GB. I choose the schedule and press Save myself. New matches get an email with a score and reason. The first check is silent.”

**30 s NL:** “Ik zocht telkens opnieuw naar dezelfde Mac mini. Nu beschrijf ik het item, kies ik mijn budget en schema, en bevestig ik Save. Een latere nieuwe match komt met score en reden per mail. De eerste check is stil; de app kan matches missen. De eerste 100 accounts krijgen 30 dagen gratis met €1 AI-budget. Bekijk de actuele beschikbaarheid.”

**30 s EN:** “I kept searching for the same Mac mini. Now I describe the item, choose a budget and schedule, and confirm Save. A later new match arrives by email with a score and reason. The first check is silent; the app can miss matches. First 100 accounts get 30 days free with a €1 AI budget. Check live availability.”
