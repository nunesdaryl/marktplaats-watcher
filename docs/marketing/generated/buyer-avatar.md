# Buyer avatar v2: interview hypotheses (10 October 2026)

These are **hypotheses, not observed segments**. Recruit across them, then revise after interviews. The named items are real [evaluation watches](../../../evals/report.md), not evidence that each persona exists in the user base.

| Hypothesis | Job and example | Likely concern to ask about | First useful action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Specific-item deal hunter | Find a Mac mini with 16GB under €500. | Would a missed good listing or a wrong-spec alert matter more? | Set title phrase, ceiling and great-only bar; inspect first alert. |
| Budget parent | Track a Switch OLED within a fixed budget. | Does a “bidding from” price at the cap feel misleading? | Set maximum price and review price type before acting. |
| Reseller-curious buyer | Track repeatable items such as a Gazelle bicycle. | Is a 15-minute minimum too slow for their use? | Try a watch, then compare alert relevance to their manual checks. |

Do not assume age, income, family status, savings, resale income, or frequency. Ask interviewees what they sought, how they searched before, what the first alert did, and whether they would keep using the app.
