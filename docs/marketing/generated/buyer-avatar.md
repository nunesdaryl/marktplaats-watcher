## 1. Demographics

- **Name:** Joris
- **Age:** 31
- **Short description:** Works a hybrid office job, rents a flat in Utrecht, earns decently but hates overpaying.
  Wants a Mac mini with 16GB for a side project, budget €500, and his old bike was stolen so he's also half-looking
  for a Gazelle within cycling distance. He has the Marktplaats app on his home screen and checks it on the toilet,
  in meetings and before bed. He's tired of it. `[NEEDS REVIEW: persona details are illustrative, not sourced]`

## 2. Core Problem

"I know exactly what I want and roughly what it should cost. I just can't keep staring at Marktplaats all day, and
when I stop looking, the good one goes to someone else. And when I do get the saved-search e-mail, it's yesterday's
news and half of it is junk."

## 3. Top 5 Strongest Emotions

1. **Irritation at the noise.** Searching "mac mini" and scrolling past chargers, cases, business listings and, once,
   a Cisco network switch. Every irrelevant hit is a small tax on his attention.
2. **Low-level anxiety of missing out.** "Alleen krijg je helaas niet instant een melding helaas." He knows the good
   ones go fast and the daily e-mail comes too late.
3. **Guilt about wasted time.** Opening the app for the 12th time today and knowing it's a habit, not a strategy.
4. **Distrust.** Of sellers (scams), of traders posing as private sellers, and of random "alert apps" that want his
   e-mail and €20 a month.
5. **Quiet pride when he gets it right.** Paying a fair price for exactly the right spec is a small win he tells
   friends about.

## 4. Top 5 Biggest Fears

1. Finding the perfect 16GB M2 for €420, messaging within the hour, and hearing "sorry, al verkocht". Again.
2. Settling for the 8GB model at a worse price because he got impatient, and regretting it every time it lags.
3. Paying for an alert subscription he forgets to cancel, for an item he bought three weeks ago.
4. Getting scammed on a "too good to be true" listing he rushed into because he was afraid of missing it.
5. Being the guy who spent more hours hunting a bargain than the €80 he saved was worth.

## 5. How These Fears Affect Key Relationships

1. **Partner:** she sees him scrolling Marktplaats at dinner and sighs. He puts the phone face down, then picks it
   up again when she goes to the kitchen.
2. **Friends:** one friend always "just happens" to find great deals. Joris quietly compares himself.
3. **Colleagues:** he checks the app in stand-ups; someone noticed. Small, but embarrassing.
4. **Sellers:** he's curt and fast in messages because he's afraid of losing the item, which reads as pushy.
5. **Himself:** he knows it's a silly amount of mental space for a second-hand computer, and that annoys him more.

## 6. 5 Painful Things People Might Say

- **Partner:** "Just buy a new one. You've been looking for three weeks."
- **Friend (well-meaning):** "Ah man, I saw one for €380 on Tuesday. Didn't you get it?"
- **Colleague:** "Are you on Marktplaats again?"
- **Seller:** "Sorry, al verkocht. Er waren veel reacties."
- **Himself at midnight:** "You could have saved the hours and paid the €100 extra."

2. **Marktplaats saved search.** "One e-mail a day, at the time I saved it. By then it's gone, and it's full of stuff I
   didn't mean."
3. **A free alert app trial.** "Pinged me every few minutes with every charger and case. Muted it by day two."

## 8. What They Don't Want To Do

1. "I'm not paying a monthly subscription for a one-off purchase."
2. "I'm not installing yet another app that pings me all day."
3. "I'm not going to fill in a 12-field alert form. I know what I want, let me just say it."
4. "I'm not self-hosting a bot."
5. "I'm not buying new at full price. That's the whole point."

## 9. Primary Transformation (Magic Genie)

1. "I get one e-mail at 8 in the morning with two listings, both actually what I asked for, and a line saying why."
2. "I stopped opening Marktplaats. If something good shows up, I'll hear about it."
3. "It told me the €450 one was 9/10: 16GB, M2, 5 km away. I messaged, went that evening, done."
4. "No junk. No chargers, no switches, no traders."
5. "When I'm done, I pause the watch and forget about it. No subscription to cancel."

## 10. How Transformation Affects Relationships

- **Reputation:** "the guy who got an M2 Mac mini for €420".
- **Attraction/home life:** phone stays in his pocket at dinner. His partner notices.
- **How others see him:** organised, calm, in control of his time.

## 12. Market-Specific Information

**A. What does the market pin success on?**
Speed. "Whoever messages first gets it." Most competitors reinforce this belief ("direct", "every 3 minutes"). Our job
is to add a second belief: *knowing which one is worth messaging about* matters as much as being quick.

**B. What must the market give up by solving the problem?**
The little dopamine hit of checking the app, and the feeling of "hunting". Some people enjoy the hunt. The product
must feel like a helpful friend, not like it takes the game away.

**C. Who does the market blame?**
- External: Marktplaats (late alerts, promoted and business listings: "Marktplaats zonder spam" extensions exist),
  traders and resellers who snipe everything, scammers.
- Internal: "I wasn't quick enough", "I should have checked in the evening".

**D. Top 5 biggest objections (inner voice)**
1. "Why not just save the search on Marktplaats?"
2. "Every 15 minutes at best? The good ones are gone in 5."
3. "Who's behind this? Is it a scam using Marktplaats' name?"
4. "Will the AI hide a good listing from me?"
5. "It's a student project. Will it still work next month?"
