# Competitor Analysis — Marktplaats saved search ("Bewaarde zoekopdrachten")

> `04-researcher /analyseer-concurrent` format. Researched 2026-09-27. Sources in ../references/research.md.

## Overview
- **Website:** https://www.marktplaats.nl (help: https://help.marktplaats.nl/s/article/beheren-opgeslagen-zoekopdrachten, did not render for the fetch tool)
- **Industry:** Dutch classifieds (largest in NL)
- **Audience:** every Marktplaats buyer
- **Positioning:** built-in convenience: save a search, get told about new ads

## Offer
- **Products/services:** saved searches with e-mail notification
- **Price:** free
- **Unique mechanism:** none; it is the platform's own search, saved
- **Guarantee:** n/a

## Marketing & Content
- 2026 campaign "Ook slim" focuses on AI for sellers ("Supersnelle advertenties", Emerce, 12 Jan 2026). A blog reports natural-language search: "Nieuwe zoekfuncties begrijpen gewone taal." (aiinsider.nl, 3 Feb 2026). `[NEEDS REVIEW: verify this is live, not announced]`

## Strengths
Official, free, full result coverage, no ToS risk, already in every buyer's app.

## Weaknesses / Opportunities
- Reported as a once-a-day e-mail at the time of saving ("Alleen krijg je helaas niet instant een melding helaas.", Viafora 2024; "as far as I can tell that's a day overview", GitHub README).
- No relevance scoring or explanation; promoted/business listings annoy users enough that "Marktplaats zonder spam" / "Cleanplaats" extensions exist.
- Push behaviour for saved searches is contested between sources. `[NEEDS REVIEW]`

## Customer Sentiment
Works, but late and repetitive ("En constant hetzelfde plaatje in de melding", Viafora 2024).
