# Competitor Analysis — Marktplaats saved search ("Bewaarde zoekopdrachten")

> `04-researcher /analyseer-concurrent` format. Researched 2026-09-27. Sources in ../references/research.md.

## Overview
- **Website:** https://www.marktplaats.nl (help: https://help.marktplaats.nl/s/article/beheren-opgeslagen-zoekopdrachten plus the PC, Android and iOS variants, read in Chrome on 29 Sep 2026)
- **Industry:** Dutch classifieds (largest in NL)
- **Audience:** every Marktplaats buyer
- **Positioning:** built-in convenience: save a search, get told about new ads

## Offer
- **Products/services:** saved searches with a daily notification by push and/or e-mail
- **Price:** free
- **Unique mechanism:** none; it is the platform's own search, saved
- **Guarantee:** n/a

## Marketing & Content
- 2026 campaign "Ook slim" focuses on AI for sellers ("Supersnelle advertenties", Emerce, 12 Jan 2026). A blog reports natural-language search: "Nieuwe zoekfuncties begrijpen gewone taal." (aiinsider.nl, 3 Feb 2026). `[NEEDS REVIEW: verify this is live, not announced]`

## Strengths
Official, free, full result coverage, no ToS risk, already in every buyer's app.

## Weaknesses / Opportunities
- **Daily, verified 29 Sep 2026:** PC help "Bij nieuwe resultaten ontvang je dagelijks een melding"; Android/iOS "dagelijks een e-mail". Users report the same ("Alleen krijg je helaas niet instant een melding helaas.", Viafora 2024). The general help page says "direct een melding … als eerste", contradicting the platform pages.
- No relevance scoring or explanation; promoted/business listings annoy users enough that "Marktplaats zonder spam" / "Cleanplaats" extensions exist.
- **Channels, verified in Daryl's logged-in account:** per saved search only two switches, "Push notificaties" and "E-mail". No choice of time or frequency, no threshold.
- **Noise, measured live:** "mac mini", page 1: 25 unique listings, about 9 not a Mac mini (adapters, docks, other Macs, wanted ads); 10 paid placements, and the order is partly bought.
- Daryl's own saved search "mac mini 16gb" (e-mail on) produced no e-mails we could find in Gmail, spam and trash included.

## Customer Sentiment
Works, but late and repetitive ("En constant hetzelfde plaatje in de melding", Viafora 2024).

## Notifications observed in our own account (10 Oct 2026)
Daryl's signed-in Marktplaats account, "Meldingen" page, photographed 10 Oct 2026 around 21:20 (cropped to the notification list; no account name): [2026-10-10-marktplaats-notifications.png](evidence/2026-10-10-marktplaats-notifications.png).
- Two saved searches, "playstation 5" and "mac mini", each produced **one notification per day**, each a single round-up: "Er zijn 812 / 834 / 653 nieuwe advertenties voor je zoekopdracht: playstation 5" and "Er zijn 26 / 28 / 25 nieuwe advertenties voor je zoekopdracht: mac mini" ("ongeveer 3 uur geleden", "1 dag geleden", "2 dagen geleden"; earlier days seen 8-10 Oct).
- This matches the PC, Android and iOS help pages ("dagelijks", checked 29 Sep 2026). The general help page still says "direct ... als eerste", so any claim must say "in our own account" with the dates, never "Marktplaats only alerts once a day".
