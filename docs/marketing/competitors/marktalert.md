> `04-researcher /analyseer-concurrent` format. Researched 2026-09-27 from https://marktalert.nl/.

## Overview
- **Website:** https://marktalert.nl/ (iOS app: https://apps.apple.com/nl/app/marktalert/id6757766110)
- **Industry:** second-hand listing alerts (Marktplaats, 2dehands, Vinted)
- **Audience:** Dutch/Belgian buyers who want to be first
- **Positioning:** independent automatic alerts vs native alerts that come "maximaal één keer per dag"

## Offer
- **Products/services:** e-mail, Telegram, app push, webhooks; price-drop monitoring (paid)
- **Price:** Free (2 alerts, 15-min checks, valid 14 days), Plus €6.95/month (5 alerts, 5 min), PRO €10.95/month (10+ alerts, 3 min, 45 days)
- **Unique mechanism:** frequency; no AI relevance scoring mentioned
- **Guarantee:** free tier instead

## Marketing & Content
SEO blog ("complete gids" articles for Marktplaats and Vinted). Claims "4.8/5 Gebruikerswaardering" without visible reviews.

## Strengths
Cheap, free entry at the same 15-minute cadence Watcher offers, more platforms and channels.

## Weaknesses / Opportunities
Keyword/filter alerts only; free alerts expire after 14 days; no explanation per listing.
