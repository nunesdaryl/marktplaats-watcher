# Competitor Analysis — MPAlerts

> `04-researcher /analyseer-concurrent` format. Researched 2026-09-27 from https://mpalerts.nl/.

## Overview
- **Website:** https://mpalerts.nl/
- **Industry:** second-hand listing alerts (Marktplaats, 2dehands, Vinted, Facebook Marketplace)
- **Audience:** deal hunters and small traders (has a Business tier)
- **Positioning:** "De slimste alerts": AI that understands what you mean

## Offer
- **Products/services:** alerts via push app, e-mail, Telegram, Discord, RSS, live dashboard with audio
- **Price:** Basic €19.95/month (5 searches), Business €39.95/month (15 searches)
- **Unique mechanism:** "Our A.I. reads every listing and automatically filters irrelevant results"; filters out promoted listings
- **Guarantee:** 7-day free trial, no credit card

## Marketing & Content
Testimonial-led homepage. Not checked: ad library, social channels.

## Strengths
The same AI-relevance idea as Watcher, plus speed (seconds to minutes), more platforms and channels.

## Weaknesses / Opportunities
