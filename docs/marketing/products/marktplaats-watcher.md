# Marktplaats Watcher: founding offer (draft, 10 October 2026)

**Offer:** A place among the first 100 founding accounts gives 30 days free with a €1 AI budget per account. No card is needed at sign-up. Show only the app's live capacity count when publishing. At the €1 budget limit, watches and chat pause until the next 30-day budget period. The free period and AI budget have limits; do not describe the offer as unlimited or permanently free. See [product facts](../references/onboarding.md) and the [founding implementation](../../../frontend/convex/founding.ts).

**Promise, NL:** “Laat een watch op jouw tijden naar een specifiek tweedehands item kijken. Nieuwe advertenties boven jouw matchdrempel komen met een score en reden per e-mail.”

**Promise, EN:** “Set a watch for a specific second-hand item on your schedule. New listings above your chosen match bar reach your inbox with a score and reason.”

## Value equation applied honestly

| Driver | Offer design | Evidence / limit |
|---|---|---|
| Desired outcome | Spend less attention on repeated checks for a named item. | A product capability, not a measured time saving. |
| Perceived likelihood | Show a real watch setup, alert and the dated offline evaluation. | [10 October report](../../../evals/report.md) includes misses; no customer outcome proof. |
| Time delay | The user can save a watch immediately. | First check is silent; first alert depends on a new qualifying listing and chosen schedule. |
| Effort and sacrifice | Plain-language setup, buyer confirms Save, no card to start. | Sign-in and a watch are required; AI budget and founding period apply. |
