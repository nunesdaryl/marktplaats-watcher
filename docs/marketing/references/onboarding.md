# Context DNA: product and founder (10 October 2026)

## Business identity
Marktplaats Watcher is Daryl Nunes's independent web app, live at https://marktplaats-watcher.vercel.app. It is not affiliated with Marktplaats. A buyer describes an item in ordinary Dutch or English, reviews the proposed watch, and presses **Save**. The app checks on the chosen schedule and emails new listings that meet the chosen alert level, with a score and reason. The first check records existing listings silently; it is not an instant alert. Sources: [README](../../../README.md), [landing](../../../frontend/src/Landing.jsx), [evaluation](../../../evals/report.md).

## Founding offer
The first 100 accounts can use the app free for 30 days, with a €1 AI budget per account. There is no card at sign-up. Once the free period ends, the app asks for feedback; continued access follows the product's current founding flow, not a promised permanent free tier. See [beta implementation](../../../frontend/convex/beta.ts) and [founding flow](../../../frontend/convex/founding.ts). A [6 October outreach snapshot](../outreach-drafts-2026-10-06.md) recorded 4 of 100 places taken (96 left) then. That is historical, not the current capacity. Display the live capacity query at publication time; never reuse a dated places-left number.

## Product boundaries
Up to five watches per account. Checks can be as frequent as every 15 minutes, not instant. Users choose a match bar: great (8+), good (6+), or every new listing. A watch can include a maximum price, title phrase, postcode and radius. Rating an alert supplies feedback. The app does not contact sellers or place bids. A suggested offer or message is for the buyer to review and send. Exact limits and retention should be checked against the current UI before publishing any campaign.

## Proof and gaps
The [10 October evaluation](../../../evals/report.md) used 53 frozen real listings from six watches. At the great threshold it reports 100% precision and 74% recall, median of three runs, against human-corrected labels. This is an offline sample, not a customer outcome or reliability promise. Its review queue has zero pending and zero confirmed scorer cases; it records no alert ratings yet. The same report records misses, including a Mac mini and Gazelle bicycle. Use those limits next to any result claim.
