## Market language

How people talk about this, which feeds copy and the `/agents` page:

- **Their words, positive:** "runs 24/7", "one paste and it builds", "connect your tools", "a directory is curated data".
- **Their words, fear:** "told people my address", "agreed a lowball price", "without even telling me", "zonder overleg".
- **Our answer, in their terms:** your agent finds and scores; you decide; nothing is sent, bought or shared without your tap.

## Personas (Strategist /avatar, short form)

1. **The agent power user.** Has Grok Bot, Claude or ChatGPT agents running daily and wants "Marktplaats" as one more connector.
   - Pain: agents that search Marktplaats re-scrape, get blocked, and flood them with noise.
   - Wants: "tell my agent what I'm hunting and get only the good ones".
   - Won't: hand an agent their Marktplaats login.
   - Where: X, Grok Bot and Cursor marketplaces, Claude plugins.
2. **The Dutch deal hunter or reseller.** Lives in the 50K-member Facebook groups and checks Marktplaats by hand.
   - Pain: refreshing, missing the good listing, scams.
   - Fear, from the Muse stories: an agent that agrees prices or gives out an address.
   - Where: Facebook groups, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, the existing founding-user funnel. The agent route is a later step for them, after the app.
3. **The developer or builder.** Building a shopping or reseller agent who needs reliable, scored Marktplaats data.
   - Today they use an unofficial MCP or an Apify scraper.
   - Wants: stable tools, OAuth, clear limits.
   - Where: MCP Registry, Smithery, GitHub.

## Offer (Strategist /offer, value equation)

- **Dream outcome:** your agent is first to the good listing, and you approve the deal.
- **Likelihood:** scored alerts with a one-line reason and stored evidence. Every figure we cite comes from the current `evals/report.md`.
- **Time delay:** one OAuth sign-in from the agent, then the first scored alert on the next check.
- **Effort:** say it in plain words to the agent you already use. There is no new app to learn.
- **Price:** the same founding offer (free for the first 30 days for the first 100 accounts, €1 AI budget). Directories forbid in-app digital sales, so any paid plan stays on our own site later.
- **Unique mechanism:** watch → score → evidence → human approval, on our servers, with no Marktplaats session ever leaving the person's browser.
- **Guarantee and safety:** nothing is sent, bid or shared on your behalf.

## Funnel by channel

`registry or marketplace listing → install → OAuth sign-in (founding place check) → first tool call (list or create a watch; ADR 0016, with an Undo e-mail) → first scored alert → rating`
