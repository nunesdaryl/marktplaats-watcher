## Interview and consent workflow
After a participant has had an alert, ask what they wanted, what they did before, whether this alert was worth a look, and what felt wrong. Ask separately for permission to publish exact words, first name and any screenshot. Record the date, channel, precise quote, watch context and permission. Until then, avoid testimonials and invented before/after stories.
