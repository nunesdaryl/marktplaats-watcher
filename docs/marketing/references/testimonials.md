## Language Patterns

### Key Phrases & Recurring Language
- "melding" / "meldingen" (7 sources), "direct" / "instant" (6), "zoekopdracht opslaan" (4), "te laat" / "vertraging"
  (4), "spam" / "cluttering" (4), "elke dag even kijken" / "refreshen" (3).

### Emotional Triggers
- **Frustration at being late** ("helaas", "flinke vertraging").
- **Annoyance at repetition and clutter** ("constant hetzelfde plaatje", "spam", "bulk trader").
- **Resignation** to manual checking ("Elke dag even kijken").
- **Pride / win** when it works ("Scored an iPhone 15 … half retail price").
- **Relief at being understood** ("understands exactly what I mean").

### Before/After Language
- Before: "elke dag even kijken", "dagelijks refreshen", "day overview", "te laat".
- After (competitor testimonials): "set up in 5 minutes", "no longer dealing with … clutter", "scored … same day".

## Collection Plan: 3–5 Testimonials From Beta Users

1. **Recruit 8–10 beta users** (expect 3–5 to reply): course peers, friends hunting a specific item this month,
   one or two Tweakers/Reddit posters who asked about Marktplaats alerts. Ask each to set up 1–2 real watches.
2. **Wait for a real alert.** Ask 7–14 days after sign-up, or right after their first e-mail with a good match.
3. **Ask three short questions** (by e-mail or DM, in their language):
   - What were you looking for, and how did you look for it before?
   - What happened with the first alert you got? (Did the score and reason match what you thought?)
   - Would you tell a friend about it? What would you say?
4. **Also ask for the negative:** "What annoyed you or felt wrong?" Irrelevant high scores are the most useful
   feedback for the product and for the LinkedIn story.
5. **Get permission** to quote with first name + city (or anonymised), and to screenshot the alert (with the listing
   title only, no seller details).
6. **Record verbatim** in this file under "Customer Testimonials" and "Success Stories", dated, with the watch that
   produced it. Re-run `/avatar` after three are in.
