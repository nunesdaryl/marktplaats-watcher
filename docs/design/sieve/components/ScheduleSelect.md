# ScheduleSelect

The schedule editor's pills: native `<select>` elements styled as `tag` pills inside the sentence "Check Marktplaats [every 15 minutes ▾] and e-mail me [good matches ▾]".

- 36px high, `radius-sm`, `tag` / `tag-ink`, Geist 600 17px, a 14px chevron at 70%.
- **Box:** `surface`, radius 8px, 16px padding. The help text sits in `elevated` at `text-sm`; the footnote is `text-2`.
- **States:** hover mixes 12% `tag-ink` in; open/focus shows the focus ring; disabled 40%.
- The notify options keep their copy: great matches only (8–10), good matches (6–10), every new listing.
