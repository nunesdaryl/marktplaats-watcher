# ProposalCard

The AI's drafted watch. The AI only proposes; the person presses **Save watch**.

- `elevated`, 1px `line`, radius 8px, `shadow`, max 560px wide. A 4px `tag` bar runs along the card's top edge (it replaces today's left bar).
- **Body:** the fill-in sentence at `text-lg` with `tag` pills.
- **Actions:** primary "Save watch" and plain "Adjust".
- **While drafting:** the status line "Drafting a watch for you to check…" in `text-2`, with a shimmer that is off under reduced motion.
