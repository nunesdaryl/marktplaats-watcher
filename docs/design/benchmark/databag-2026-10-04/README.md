# UI/UX benchmark: Vinod's Academy community

**The useful lesson is orientation.** The community site makes the next action visible through grouped navigation, descriptive account choices, progress summaries and short explanations near unfamiliar controls. Marktplaats Watcher can use that clarity in a few places without changing its calmer Sieve design. The highest-value work is clearer account choices and more actionable first-use guidance. This is an idea audit, not a design or implementation approval.

Source: [Vinod's Academy community](https://community.databag.ai), observed read-only by the station on 4 October 2026. Evidence is the dated [station notes](notes.md); the browser tool could not save screenshots, so there are no image files in this benchmark. The member's name and e-mail are omitted. Judgments about why a pattern works and how it might fit our app are **our inferences**, not measured outcomes. No source code, assets, logo, colour palette, fonts or long-form copy are reproduced.

## Fit for Marktplaats Watcher

“Adopt” means reuse the interaction idea; “Adapt” means reshape it for our tasks and existing components. Every proposed implementation would use [our design system](../../DESIGN-SYSTEM.md): Geist, warm graphite surfaces, teal `--accent`, and lilac `--tag` only for its existing meanings.

| Element | Call and reason | Our screen and design-system mapping |
|---|---|---|
| Sign-in split hero and card | **Skip.** Our landing already explains the product with a concrete scored example; a second full hero and custom credential form would add weight to a short sign-in path. | `frontend/src/Landing.jsx` keeps its existing `--display`, `--tag`, `--accent` and Clerk modal path ([type/colour rules](../../DESIGN-SYSTEM.md#3-typography)). |
| Sidebar groups and collapse | **Adapt.** Clearer grouping could help a long watch/chat list; collapse needs a real density problem before adding a control. | `frontend/src/components/Sidebar.jsx`: reuse `.nav-section`, `.nav-item`, `--sidebar`, `--fill-strong` and `--sidebar-w`, with the phone tab bar retained ([layout](../../DESIGN-SYSTEM.md#9-layout-and-breakpoints)). |
| Top bar search and actions | **Skip as a new bar.** Desktop already has `⌘K` search in `Sidebar.jsx`; phones have a top bar, theme and account control in `App.jsx`. An Ask button would duplicate the chat entry point. | Keep `frontend/src/App.jsx` / `Sidebar.jsx` on `--glass`, `--accent` and existing icon buttons ([components](../../DESIGN-SYSTEM.md#7-components)). |
| Described account actions | **Adapt.** The identity-first, explained-action pattern could clarify where profile, theme and privacy controls lead. Clerk currently supplies the account menu, with a separate app theme reset item. | `frontend/src/components/AccountButton.jsx`, `Sidebar.jsx`, `App.jsx`; reuse `.menu`, `--elevated`, `--text-2`, `--radius-lg`, `--shadow` and [`--touch`](../../DESIGN-SYSTEM.md#4-space-radius-and-elevation). Do not imply the member has a role or permission choice we do not offer. |
| Home dashboard | **Adapt.** Progress and next-step modules suit an owner dashboard only where the numbers link to records. Personal learning and event modules do not fit the watcher. | `frontend/src/views/admin/Overview.jsx`: preserve clickable `.stat.door`; use `--font-mono` for counts and `--line` / `--shadow` per [component rules](../../DESIGN-SYSTEM.md#7-components). |
| Feed and cards | **Skip the social feed.** Watcher is about finding and rating listings, not publishing posts. The filter/search pattern already has close equivalents. | `frontend/src/views/AlertsView.jsx` and `components/ListingCard.jsx`: retain listing-card links, score reasons, `.chip`, `--accent-soft` and [card rules](../../DESIGN-SYSTEM.md#7-components). |

| Video explainer and progress | **Adapt.** A compact “how watching works” explanation could help first-use watch setup; viewing-time and course-tree controls do not fit. | `frontend/src/views/Onboarding.jsx`, `views/WatchesView.jsx`: use `.grouped`/`.empty-note`, `--space-*`, `--text-sm` and `--accent` for the next action ([states](../../DESIGN-SYSTEM.md#8-states)). |

## Top five changes worth building

Ranked by expected user value relative to implementation effort. These are **ready-to-file issue drafts**, not work in MW-77. Evidence for the source pattern is linked in each item; the exact user benefit remains a hypothesis until tested.

1. **Explain account destinations before selection.** Problem: the sidebar account row opens Clerk, while privacy and archive sit beside it, so related destinations are split (`Sidebar.jsx`, `AccountButton.jsx`; [source account note](notes.md#shell-and-measured-styles)). Acceptance criteria: present a short description for each available account-related destination; preserve Clerk's actual security/sign-out actions and the current theme behavior; keyboard, touch and screen-reader operation remain usable. Files: `frontend/src/components/AccountButton.jsx`, `frontend/src/components/Sidebar.jsx`, `frontend/src/App.jsx`, `frontend/src/styles.css`. Use `--elevated`, `--text-2`, `--line`; validate what Clerk supports before choosing the implementation.
2. **Make first-use empty states actionable.** Problem: “None yet” in the sidebar gives no path, while the Alerts page has a good explanation but no direct watch action (`Sidebar.jsx`, `AlertsView.jsx`; [source zero-state notes](notes.md#home)). Acceptance criteria: first-use Watches and Alerts states say what appears and when; offer one clear create-watch action where relevant; no extra action on populated states; preserve the single-sentence empty-state voice rule. Files: `frontend/src/components/Sidebar.jsx`, `frontend/src/views/WatchesView.jsx`, `frontend/src/views/AlertsView.jsx`, `frontend/src/styles.css`. Use `.empty-note`, `--text-2`, `--accent`.
3. **Explain the watch lifecycle where setup starts.** Problem: a new user may not know why the first check is quiet or how scores turn into e-mails (`Landing.jsx`, `Onboarding.jsx`; [source video explainer note](notes.md#videos-videos)). Acceptance criteria: a short, collapsible explanation near first watch setup covers initial baseline, check schedule, scoring and alert threshold in our words; stays out of the way after setup; works by keyboard and on phones. Files: `frontend/src/views/Onboarding.jsx`, `frontend/src/components/WatchSheet.jsx`, `frontend/src/styles.css`. Use `--space-*`, `--text-sm`, `--line` and existing disclosure styling.
4. **Give the owner dashboard a next-action summary.** Problem: its many clickable metrics make inspection possible but do not point to the most urgent review first (`Overview.jsx`; [source home note](notes.md#home)). Acceptance criteria: show a small set of existing, actionable review counts at the top; each opens the matching existing drilldown; zero states are explicit; no new metric or data fetch. Files: `frontend/src/views/admin/Overview.jsx`, `frontend/src/styles.css`. Use `.stat.door`, `--font-mono`, `--line` and `--accent` only for the action.
5. **Test feedback entry-point discoverability.** Problem: the source reinforces Feedback in the shell and feed copy; our entry points are the desktop sidebar and beta banner ([source shell/feed notes](notes.md#shell-and-measured-styles), `Sidebar.jsx`, `App.jsx`). Acceptance criteria: measure whether users can find Feedback on desktop and phone; if they cannot, add one context-safe entry point that opens the existing `FeedbackSheet`; verify it does not cover cards, tab bar or sheet controls. Files: `frontend/src/components/Sidebar.jsx`, `frontend/src/App.jsx`, `frontend/src/views/FeedbackSheet.jsx`, `frontend/src/styles.css`. Use `--touch`, `--z-bars`, `--accent` and existing button styles.

## What we already do better

- **Scores explain themselves.** `ListingCard.jsx` and `AlertsView.jsx` show a `0–10` score and its reason, and the landing gives a real scored example. The observed community cards offer status and engagement context, but no analogous decision explanation ([feed note](notes.md#feed-feed); [our card rules](../../DESIGN-SYSTEM.md#7-components)).
- **Feedback closes into a review loop.** `FeedbackSheet.jsx` can attach a privacy-filtered page screenshot and lets users rate alerts; `Overview.jsx` exposes review records. The source observation establishes a visible Feedback route, not what happens after submission ([shell note](notes.md#shell-and-measured-styles); [our feedback loop](../../../system-design.html)).
- **Our visual rules are explicit.** Teal action semantics, lilac highlight, Geist type, spacing, states, contrast and plain-language rules are documented and checked against `styles.css` ([design system](../../DESIGN-SYSTEM.md)). The source's measured styles are useful reference observations, not a claim about its internal design governance ([measurements](notes.md#shell-and-measured-styles)).
